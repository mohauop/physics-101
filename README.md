## Course Overview
This course provides a comprehensive understanding into physics in a clear visuals and simulations . We will transition from classical mechanics to quantum mechanics to nuclear physics and particle physics and cosmology and relativity.
We will dive into the simulations and codes provided by the most advanced experiments .
